## Quick Reference

### What is Last Call?
A food rescue marketplace. Restaurants list surplus food as discounted bags. Customers buy and pick them up. Last Call takes 20%.

### Where does it operate?
Launching in Karachi, Pakistan. Expanding to Lahore and Islamabad within 12 months.

### Who are the users?
- **Customers:** Urban Pakistanis looking for quality food at 50–70% off
- **Partners:** Restaurants, bakeries, cafés with daily food surplus
- **Admins:** Last Call operations team

### What are the three apps?
- **Last Call** — Customer mobile app (iOS + Android)
- **Last Call Partner** — Restaurant/café management app (iOS + Android)
- **Last Call Admin** — Internal operations web dashboard

### How does the money work?
Customer pays → Last Call keeps 20% → Partner gets 80% every Monday via JazzCash.

### What's the north star metric?
**Bags saved per month** — the number that proves both supply and demand are working.
