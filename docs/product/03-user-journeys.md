# Last Call — User Journeys

> Step-by-step walkthroughs of how each type of user experiences Last Call, from first discovery through repeat use.

---

## Journey 1 — Customer: First-Time Purchase

### Stage 1: Discovery
- Hamza hears about Last Call from a colleague who saved money at a restaurant
- He searches "Last Call Pakistan" and finds the app on Google Play Store
- Downloads the app (under 20 MB)

### Stage 2: Sign Up
- App opens to a green welcome screen with the tagline: *"Save food. Save money."*
- He enters his Pakistani phone number: `0312-3456789`
- Receives a 6-digit OTP via SMS within seconds
- Enters OTP — account created instantly. No forms, no passwords, no email required.

### Stage 3: Browsing
- Home screen shows available bags near him, filtered by his area (Gulshan)
- He sees area chips at the top: `All | Burns Road | DHA | Clifton | Gulshan | Saddar`
- Selects **Gulshan**
- Sees 8 available bags from different restaurants — biryani, bakery, café
- Each card shows: restaurant name, bag title, original price crossed out, discounted price, bags left, pickup window

### Stage 4: Choosing a Bag
- He taps on "Desi Surprise Box" from Al-Madina Restaurant
- Bag detail screen shows:
  - Photo of the bag/food
  - Restaurant name and rating (⭐ 4.3)
  - Description: *"A mix of whatever's left — usually biryani, karahi, and a dessert. No two bags are the same!"*
  - Original value: Rs. 450, Discounted price: **Rs. 140** (-69%)
  - Pickup window: **8:30 PM – 9:30 PM tonight**
  - Pickup address with map link
  - 🌿 CO₂ saved: 2.5 kg
  - 3 bags left

### Stage 5: Reserving
- Taps **"Reserve Bag"** button
- Confirmation: payment method selector appears
  - Cash (pay at pickup)
  - JazzCash
  - Easypaisa
  - SadaPay
  - NayaPay
  - Raast
- He selects **JazzCash** and is redirected to JazzCash's payment screen
- Completes payment in 20 seconds
- Returns to Last Call — order confirmed screen

### Stage 6: Pickup
- Order screen shows his **6-digit pickup code**: `H7K2M9`
- Status: **CONFIRMED** → will change to **READY** when restaurant packs the bag
- He gets a **WhatsApp message**: *"Your Last Call order from Al-Madina is confirmed! Pickup code: H7K2M9. Pickup 8:30–9:30 PM at [address]."*
- At 8 PM he gets a push notification: *"Your bag is ready for pickup!"*
- He goes to the restaurant, shows the cashier his phone with the code
- Cashier enters the code into their app → verified
- Cashier hands over the bag. Done.

### Stage 7: Post-Pickup
- Notification: *"How was your Last Call experience? Leave a review for Al-Madina!"*
- He rates the experience ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ and writes: *"Biryani was amazing, huge portion for Rs. 140"*
- His profile now shows: **1 meal saved, 2.5 kg CO₂ prevented**

**Total time from app open to bag reserved: under 3 minutes.**

## Journey 2 — Customer: Repeat Usage Pattern

After the first purchase, Hamza's behaviour changes:

**Week 1–2:** Buys 2–3 times. Tries different restaurants. Starts comparing value across bags.

**Week 3+:** Develops a routine. Opens the app at 6 PM on his way home. Has 2–3 "favourite" restaurants saved. Checks if they've listed bags today.

**Monthly habits:**
- Adds frequent restaurants to Favourites for quick access
- Uses the **Explore** tab to discover new areas when visiting friends
- Shares his referral code with a colleague → earns a discount on his next purchase
- Personal impact counter is now: *42 meals saved, 105 kg CO₂ prevented* — he shares a screenshot on WhatsApp

## Journey 3 — Partner: Onboarding

### Stage 1: Discovery
- Usman's neighbour (another restaurant owner) tells him about Last Call
- A Last Call field sales rep visits Burns Road and explains the concept in 5 minutes
- Usman downloads the **Last Call Partner** app

### Stage 2: Registration (Step 1 — Business Info)
- Opens partner app → Login with phone OTP (same as customer)
- First screen: *"Let's set up your business"*
- Fills in:
  - Business name: Al-Madina Restaurant
  - Category: Restaurant
  - Area: Burns Road
  - Full address
  - Pickup instructions: "Come to the main counter and show your code"
- Taps **Next**

### Stage 3: Registration (Step 2 — Documents)
- *"We need to verify your identity before you go live"*
- Uploads photo of CNIC (required)
- Optionally uploads business license
- Taps **Submit for Review**

### Stage 4: Registration (Step 3 — Pending)
- Screen shows: *"Application submitted! Our team reviews within 24–48 hours."*
- Checkmarks show: Business details saved ✓, Documents uploaded ✓, Pending admin review ✓
- Goes back to his restaurant. Waits.

### Stage 5: Approval
- Next day, Bilal (admin) reviews his documents and taps **Approve**
- Usman receives:
  - Push notification: *"🎉 You're approved! Start listing bags now."*
  - WhatsApp: *"Your Last Call partner account is approved. Welcome aboard!"*
  - Email: formal welcome with getting-started guide
- Partner app dashboard unlocks

### Stage 6: First Bag Listing
- Dashboard shows today's stats (all zeros)
- Taps **"+ Create New Bag"**
- Fills in:
  - Title: "Desi Surprise Box"
  - Description: "A mix of leftovers — usually biryani, karahi, and a dessert"
  - Original price: Rs. 450
  - Discounted price: Rs. 140
  - Quantity: 5 bags available
  - Pickup date: Today
  - Pickup window: 8:30 PM – 9:30 PM
  - Tags: biryani, karahi, desi
- Taps **"Create & Publish Bag"**
- Bag goes live immediately and appears in customer search results

**Time to list first bag: under 2 minutes.**

## Journey 4 — Partner: Daily Operations

**7:30 PM — Assessing surplus:**
Usman looks at what's left in the kitchen. About Rs. 2,000 worth of biryani and karahi. He decides to make 5 bags.

**7:35 PM — Creating the bag:**
Opens partner app → "Create New Bag" → fills in details → published. Takes 90 seconds.

**7:40 PM — First order arrives:**
Push notification: *"New Order! Customer code: X4T7P2"*
Staff see the incoming order on the Orders tab.

**8:00 PM — Preparing bags:**
Orders tab shows 4 orders confirmed. Staff start packing. Cashier taps **"Mark Ready"** on each order when packed.

**8:30 PM — Customers arrive:**
Customers show their phone screens with pickup codes.
Cashier taps **Verify Pickup** → types in the 6-digit code → ✓ Verified → hands over the bag.
For cash customers: confirms cash received → order marked complete.

**9:30 PM — Wrap up:**
All 5 bags picked up. Dashboard shows:
- Today's orders: 5
- Today's earnings: Rs. 700 (5 × Rs. 140 × 80%)

**Monday Morning — Payout:**
WhatsApp: *"Your Last Call payout of Rs. 4,200 has been sent to JazzCash 0312-xxxxxxx."*

## Journey 5 — Admin: Partner Approval Workflow

**9:00 AM — Morning routine:**
Bilal opens Last Call Admin on his laptop.
Dashboard shows: 3 pending partner approvals, 142 orders yesterday, Rs. 28,000 GMV.

**Checking pending approvals:**
Clicks Partners → filters by PENDING → sees 3 new applications.

**Reviewing Application 1 (Approved):**
- Business name: Zam Zam Bakers, Burns Road
- Category: Bakery
- Documents: CNIC uploaded ✓, Business license uploaded ✓
- CNIC photo looks legitimate
- Clicks **Approve**
- Owner immediately receives WhatsApp + push notification

**Reviewing Application 2 (Needs more info):**
- Documents: Only CNIC uploaded, blurry photo
- Clicks **Suspend** temporarily → sends WhatsApp manually asking for clearer photo
*(Future: add "Request More Info" status)*

**Reviewing Application 3 (Rejected):**
- Business name doesn't match CNIC
- Clicks **Suspend** → adds note
- Calls the number to verify before making a final decision

**Rest of morning:**
- Checks Orders tab for any cancelled or disputed orders
- Looks at weekly payout summary — Monday, so runs payout: clicks **Run Payout Now**
- 47 partners receive their settlements. Status changes to PROCESSING.
- By end of day, confirms all sent → marks as COMPLETED.

## Journey 6 — Customer: Cancellation & Refund

**Scenario:** Aisha reserved a bag at a café in DHA but can't make it to the pickup window.

1. Opens the order in her Orders tab
2. Sees **Cancel Order** option (only available if status is still CONFIRMED or READY)
3. Taps Cancel → confirms
4. Order status changes to CANCELLED
5. If paid digitally: receives notification — *"Your refund of Rs. 180 has been initiated. It will appear in your SadaPay account within 3–5 business days."*
6. If cash: no refund needed (she didn't pay yet)

**On the partner side:**
- Usman's app shows the order as CANCELLED
- The bag quantity automatically increments back (+1 available)
- He can now sell that bag to another customer

## Journey 7 — Partner: Analytics Review (Weekly)

Every Sunday, Sana (bakery owner) reviews her week's performance on the Analytics tab:

- **Total orders this week:** 34
- **Revenue earned:** Rs. 8,500
- **Top performing bag:** "Weekend Pastry Box" — 18 sold
- **Underperforming bag:** "Chocolate Cake Slice" — only 3 sold
- **Bar chart:** shows orders by bag type

**Decision she makes:** Next week she'll create more "Weekend Pastry Box" inventory and reduce the "Chocolate Cake Slice" quantity. She also notices orders drop off on Tuesday — she'll reduce her listing quantity on Tuesdays.

## Journey Summary: Key Moments That Define the Product

| Moment | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| **Customer signs up** | Must be under 60 seconds with phone OTP — every extra step loses users |
| **First bag reserved** | The "wow" moment — food at 70% off from a restaurant they know |
| **Pickup experience** | Must be smooth, non-embarrassing — the 6-digit code makes it feel premium |
| **Partner lists first bag** | Must take under 2 minutes — if it takes longer, they won't do it again |
| **Partner gets first payout** | The moment they trust the platform — must be on time, every time |
| **Customer's CO₂ counter grows** | Emotional hook that builds long-term retention and word-of-mouth |
| **Partner sees analytics** | The moment they go from passive to strategic — drives bag quality improvement |
