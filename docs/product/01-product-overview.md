# Last Call — Product Overview

> **One-line pitch:** Last Call rescues surplus food from restaurants and bakeries in Pakistan and sells it to customers at 50–70% off — before it ends up in the trash.

---

## The Problem

Every night across Karachi, hundreds of restaurants, bakeries, and cafés close with unsold food they cannot keep till the next day. That food goes straight into the bin.

At the same time, millions of Pakistanis cannot afford to eat out — but could easily afford the same food at half the price.

This is a double waste: food waste and a missed economic opportunity. Pakistan wastes approximately **40% of its food supply annually**, making it one of the highest food waste nations in the world. The economic cost exceeds **Rs. 1 trillion per year**.

---

## The Solution

**Last Call** is a mobile marketplace that connects food businesses with nearby customers in the final hours before closing.

**How it works in 3 steps:**

```
1. PARTNER LISTS    →    A restaurant packs leftover food into a "bag"
                         and lists it on Last Call at a discounted price.
                         E.g. Rs. 500 worth of biryani + sides for Rs. 150.

2. CUSTOMER BUYS    →    A customer nearby sees the bag on the app,
                         reserves it, and pays (cash or mobile payment).
                         They get a 6-digit pickup code.

3. CUSTOMER PICKS UP →   Customer shows up before closing, shows the code,
                         takes the bag. Food saved. Money saved.
```

---

## Vision

**To become Pakistan's largest food rescue platform — making surplus food accessible to every Pakistani while eliminating restaurant food waste.**

---

## Mission

Last Call's mission is to build a habit-forming marketplace where saving food is as easy and rewarding as ordering food. Every bag sold is a meal that didn't go to waste and a family that ate well for less.

---

## Value Proposition

### For Customers
- Get quality restaurant food at **50–70% off** regular prices
- Discover new restaurants and bakeries in your area
- Know the environmental impact of every purchase (CO₂ saved, meals rescued)
- Simple phone OTP login — no passwords to remember
- Pay your way: cash, JazzCash, Easypaisa, SadaPay, NayaPay, or Raast

### For Partner Restaurants & Bakeries
- **Recover revenue** from food that would otherwise be thrown away
- **Zero upfront cost** — Last Call only charges 20% on successful sales
- Reach new customers who would never have found you otherwise
- Simple management: list a bag in under 2 minutes
- WhatsApp notifications for every order — no app switching needed
- Weekly payouts directly to JazzCash account

### For Pakistan
- Reduces food waste at scale
- Makes quality food affordable for lower-income households
- Creates a new "late-hour economy" category for F&B businesses
- Measurable environmental impact (CO₂, water, landfill diversion)

## Why Now?

1. **Smartphone penetration** in Pakistan crossed 50% and is growing fast
2. **Mobile payments** (JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast) are mainstream
3. **Food inflation** has made consumers actively seek value alternatives
4. **ESG awareness** is growing among urban Pakistani businesses and consumers
5. **No competitor** is doing this in Pakistan — the market is wide open

| Feature | Last Call | Food Delivery Apps (Foodpanda, etc.) |
|---|---|---|
| Price | 50–70% off | Full price + delivery fee |
| Purpose | Rescue surplus food | On-demand delivery |
| Environmental angle | Yes (CO₂ tracking) | No |
| Partner benefit | Recover lost revenue | Pay per order |

| Pickup or delivery | **Pickup only** | Delivery |

Last Call is not competing with food delivery. It is creating a new category: **food rescue**.
